FAQ

A few things you might ask.

The short version: no money, clear rules and support when something goes wrong.

Do I need real money?
No. Everything in the student Marketplace is exchanged using Lions Geek Coins. No real-money payment is needed.
What if the service isn’t delivered?
Open a report with the service details. The team can review the exchange, contact both students and correct the transaction if needed.
How do I earn more Coins?
Complete services, log in daily, maintain your streak and finish learning missions linked to your activity.
How do I report a problem?
Visit Report & Suivi, choose the issue type and share the relevant details. You’ll receive an email reply and can track progress there.
